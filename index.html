        HOST
        
Starts the mode picked on the PLAY tab as an online game (Solo vs Bots becomes an online battle with your friends;
          Free Build and Explore are co-op). Share the room code - friends can join from anywhere.

        
      

      

        JOIN
        
        
      

      
Online play needs an internet connection: players find each other through the free PeerJS matchmaking
        service, then play peer-to-peer. Single-player modes stay fully offline. Your character travels with you.
